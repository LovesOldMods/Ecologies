# Ecologies

An ecology mod for NeoForge 1.21.1 and 26.3, based on Klei Wright's tundra and bat videos.

## Features

- Tundra and lichen woodland biomes, with coloured mosses, shrubs, dwarfed spruces and greenstone belts
- Sprouted glow lichen that slowly breaks down the rock it grows on
- Muskox that graze on lichen, stick together in herds and ram whatever goes after their calves
- Bat colonies that roost in caves, feed over your crops at night and leave guano behind
- Mushroom farms for growing fungus with guano
- Crops that bolt if they're left ripe too long
- Predators that hunt when they're hungry, stalk and wear down their prey, and take a break after

Most of it can be changed with datapacks. See [DATAPACKS.md](DATAPACKS.md).
